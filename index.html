TECH NEXUS

Traditional and Decentralised Economic System

Absence Of Digital Services And Documentation System For Heritage Touism-Based Micro Enterprises In Mysuru

The Problem

Heritage micro-enterprises in Mysuru lack structured digital documentation.

Without verified digital presence, they remain invisible to modern travelers.

Heavy dependence on middlemen leads to income loss and reduced trust.

Cultural businesses operate informally without data, analytics, or growth tools.

Our Solution

Digital Identity A dedicated platform to document and verify traditional micro-enterprises.

Direct Engagement A "Partner Dashboard" that allows artisans to manage their services and visibility.

AI Integration An AI Heritage Guide to narrate the "soul" of each craft, making history accessible and engaging.

Core Features

Digital Heritage Ledger

A professional profile for artisans to document their lineage and craft.

Interactive Heritage Map

Real-time spatial exploration of verified traditional shops and workshops.

AI-Powered Chatbot

Personalized recommendations using AI to bridge the communication gap.

Partner Command Center

Simple tools for local business owners to manage bookings and events.
